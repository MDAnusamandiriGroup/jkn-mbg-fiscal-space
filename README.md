# JKN-MBG fiscal-space analysis

Reproducibility repository for the manuscript:

**Competing Social Priorities under Fiscal Constraints: A Probabilistic Counterfactual Analysis of Nutrition Spending and Universal Health Coverage in Indonesia**

## Overview

This project evaluates how much fiscal space could be generated by alternative efficiency levels in the non-food component of Indonesia's Makan Bergizi Gratis (MBG) programme, and the probability that transferable savings could offset financing gaps in Jaminan Kesehatan Nasional (JKN).

The analysis is a **probabilistic fiscal counterfactual**, not a forecast and not a causal estimate of the effects of reducing MBG expenditure.

The analytic workflow is:

1. reconstruct monthly JKN contribution and benefit-expenditure flows from DJSN cumulative reports;
2. describe historical monthly financing patterns;
3. generate plausible 12-month JKN financing trajectories using a moving-block bootstrap;
4. propagate uncertainty in MBG non-food expenditure through Monte Carlo simulation;
5. estimate efficiency thresholds required to offset the JKN financing gap;
6. vary the proportion of savings that is actually transferable to JKN;
7. estimate counterfactual DJS Kesehatan net assets and fund resilience; and
8. repeat the analysis under a separate 2026 current-stress scenario.

## Data

`data/jkn_monthly_2024_2025.csv` contains the reconstructed monthly JKN series used in the historical analysis.

The reconstruction is based on publicly available monitoring reports from the **Dewan Jaminan Sosial Nasional (DJSN)**. Monthly flows were obtained by differencing consecutive cumulative year-to-date observations.

See [`data/data_sources.md`](data/data_sources.md) for the source documents, construction rules, MBG policy inputs, and 2026 current-stress inputs.

## Core model

Let monthly contribution revenue be `I_t` and monthly benefit expenditure be `C_t`.

The monthly JKN financing gap is:

```text
G_t = C_t - I_t
```

A positive value indicates benefit expenditure greater than contribution revenue.

Potential MBG fiscal savings are defined as:

```text
S = B × s × e
```

where:

- `B` = baseline MBG budget;
- `s` = non-food expenditure share;
- `e` = counterfactual non-food efficiency level.

Only a fraction `r` of gross savings may be transferable to JKN:

```text
F = S × r
```

The primary outcome is:

```text
P(F >= G_12)
```

where `G_12` is a simulated 12-month JKN financing gap.

## Historical uncertainty

The primary empirical series contains 22 monthly observations from March 2024 to December 2025.

Historical uncertainty is represented using a **moving-block bootstrap** with:

- primary block length: 3 months;
- robustness block lengths: 2, 4, and 6 months;
- horizon: 12 months;
- simulation draws: 250,000.

A fixed random seed is used for reproducibility.

## Monte Carlo policy assumptions

The baseline MBG allocation is Rp248 trillion.

The documented non-food share is bounded by:

- 33.33% for the Rp15,000 cost structure;
- 38.46% for the Rp13,000 cost structure.

The realised national mixture is treated as uncertain:

```text
s ~ Uniform(0.3333, 0.3846)
```

Efficiency is evaluated from 0% to 50%. The 50% scenario is an **upper-bound/high-efficiency counterfactual**, not an assumption that half of current non-food spending is waste.

Transferability scenarios are:

```text
r = {0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}
```

## Secondary outcomes

The reserve analysis distinguishes annual flow balance from accumulated financial resilience.

Counterfactual ending net assets are:

```text
A_1 = A_0 - G_12 + F
```

Fund resilience is:

```text
R = A_1 / mean monthly claims
```

The November 2025 observed baseline is Rp33.17 trillion in net assets and 2.11 months of reported fund resilience.

## 2026 current-stress scenario

The 2026 stress analysis uses reported contemporary conditions of approximately:

- Rp16-16.5 trillion monthly claims;
- Rp14 trillion monthly contributions;
- Rp2-2.5 trillion monthly financing gap.

This scenario asks what would happen **if these conditions persisted for 12 months**. It should not be interpreted as a forecast.

## Outputs

### Tables

The `outputs/tables/` directory contains machine-readable CSV outputs for:

- block-length robustness;
- efficiency thresholds by transferability;
- 2026 current-stress thresholds;
- historical net-asset and fund-resilience scenarios; and
- reserve outcomes under the 2026 current-stress scenario.

### Figures

The `outputs/figures/` directory contains final submission-quality artwork:

- Figure 1: monthly JKN financing series;
- Figure 2: 90% efficiency threshold by transferability;
- Figure 3: fund-resilience scenarios.

TIFF versions are rendered at **600 dpi** for non-photographic journal artwork, with EPS vector versions retained as masters.
